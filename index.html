Who we are

Built around the kaarigar, not around us

RepairLink started with a simple, uncomfortable observation.

Every neighbourhood already has a cobbler who can save a pair of shoes, a watch repairer who's fixed the same three brands for twenty years, a locksmith on speed-dial for every locked-out family on the street. They're skilled, they're close by, and almost nobody outside their own lane knows they exist.

Meanwhile, things that could be mended in an afternoon get thrown away, simply because finding someone trustworthy to fix them takes more effort than replacing them.

"We didn't set out to build an app. We set out to make sure the kaarigar down the street shows up when you search for one."

So RepairLink does one job well: it gives every local kaarigar a doorway onto the internet, and gives every household a fast, trustworthy way to walk through it.
