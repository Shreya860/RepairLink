About RepairLink

RepairLink was founded with a simple goal: to reduce waste and promote sustainability by making repair services accessible to everyone in North Delhi.

We connect you directly to the nearest kaarigars-cobblers, watch repairers, appliance technicians, and locksmiths. By choosing to repair instead of replace, you support local artisans and contribute to a healthier environment.

If an item truly cannot be repaired, we help coordinate its safe recycling, ensuring minimal impact on our surroundings. Join us in building a community that values craft and sustainability.
